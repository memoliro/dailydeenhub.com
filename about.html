About

DailyDeenHub is a free prayer-times app built to serve, not to sell: accurate daily prayer times, a qibla finder with compass and map, a monthly timetable (imsakiye), a Hijri calendar, a Quran reader and radio, a tasbih counter, a prayer tracker, and a finder for nearby mosques and halal places — with no account, no sign-up, and no fees.

What you can do

  • Prayer times & monthly timetable: daily times for Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha, with a full-month imsakiye.
  • Qibla finder: a live compass and a map with a line from your location to the Kaaba, plus the exact bearing and distance.
  • Quran: read the full Quran with translation, transliteration or mushaf view, listen verse by verse with the gold player, find a verse by reciting it, or listen to Quran radio.
  • Tasbih & dhikr: a classic counter and a beads mode, preset dhikrs, the 99 Names (Asma-ul Husna) and Hisnul Muslim duas.
  • Prayer tracker & qadha: check off your daily prayers, keep your streak, and track missed (qadha) prayers.
  • Khatm tracking: follow your Quran completions Juz by Juz, with as many khatms as you like.
  • Find nearby: mosques, halal restaurants and halal markets around you, shown on a map.
  • Holy days & Ramadan: kandil nights and holy days, a Ramadan hub, and extra reminders for Sahur, Tahajjud and Monday/Thursday fasts.
  • Greeting cards & baby names: ready and custom greeting cards to share, and a browsable database of Muslim baby names with meanings.
  • Reminders & prayer alerts: gentle reminders before each prayer, the adhan at prayer time, and push alerts that reach you even when the app is closed.

How prayer times are calculated

Times are calculated from your location, your time zone, the calculation method you choose (Diyanet, ISNA, Muslim World League, Umm al-Qura, Egyptian, Karachi and more) and your Asr school (Hanafi or Shafi'i). The calculation runs on your device, so prayer times and the Hijri date keep working offline.

Built to serve, not to sell

DailyDeenHub is made by a solo maker in Montreal, tested daily by family, and its religious content is reviewed for accuracy before it ships. There are no ads and no accounts, and your settings stay on your device (see our Privacy page). It works in English, Turkish and French, in a dark theme designed to be easy on the eyes, and you can install it on your phone like an app.

New here? The guides explain how prayer times are calculated, how to find the qibla with your phone, and more.

If DailyDeenHub helps you, you can support us to keep it free for everyone — or buy us a coffee.
