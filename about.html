About

Ezan Vakti by Daily Deen Hub is a free prayer-times app: accurate daily prayer times, a qibla finder with map and compass, a monthly timetable, a Quran reader and radio, and a tasbih counter.

No account, no sign-up, no fees. It works in English and Turkish, in dark and light themes, and you can install it on your phone like an app.

Prayer times are calculated using the Aladhan API. You can choose your calculation method and Asr school in Settings.

If Ezan Vakti helps you, you can buy us a coffee to keep it free for everyone.
