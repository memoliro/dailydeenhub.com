About

DailyDeenHub is a free prayer-times app: accurate daily prayer times, a qibla finder with map and compass, a monthly timetable, a Hijri calendar, a Quran reader and radio, a tasbih counter, a prayer tracker with streaks, and a finder for nearby mosques and halal restaurants.

No account, no sign-up, no fees. It works in English, Turkish, and French, in dark and light themes, and you can install it on your phone like an app.

Prayer times are calculated using the Aladhan API. You can choose your calculation method and Asr school in Settings.

If DailyDeenHub helps you, you can support us to keep it free for everyone — or buy us a coffee.
